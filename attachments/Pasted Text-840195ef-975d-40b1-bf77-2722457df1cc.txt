LAST HARVEST — STRIPEKIN FACING + IDLE ONLY
One-topic feed. Make Stripekin face movement/attack directions and play a simple idle. Do not reskin armor, retune HP/DPS, Aim/Shoot, floor tiles, Nest identity, or New Game.

Names: Stripekin = first forest chaff. Gasket = companion. GasketDev = outside assistant.

============================================================
GOAL
============================================================
Stripekin packs feel alive: units **face** the direction they move / attack, and while standing they play a light **idle** (breath / weight-shift / spear nudge) instead of frozen statues.

============================================================
MUST DO
============================================================
1) FACING
   - On move step (¼-grid): rotate yaw to face the move direction before/as the step completes.
   - On melee / spear attack: face the primary target (or attack tile) when the swing starts.
   - While aggro’d and standing (not moving / not in attack windup): prefer face **nearest threat**; else keep current facing into idle.
   - Snap or short ease (≤~150ms) — readable, not floaty. Prefer 8/4-way facing if full continuous looks wrong on iso.
   - Pack members face independently (don’t share one heading).

2) IDLE
   - When not moving / not in attack windup: loop a cheap idle — subtle breath or weight-shift + optional spear tip wobble / ear flick if bones exist.
   - Idle must not slide the feet off the ¼ cell or change occupancy.
   - Combat pause / hitstun may interrupt idle; resume after.

3) SCOPE
   - Applies to Stripekin (and alias `for_skitter` if still live id) on Nest + forest drop.
   - Reuse existing turn/step joint names if present (MAKER: anims use joint names). No new finger IK.

4) PERF
   - Lightweight: shared clip or procedural lean — no heavy blend trees / realtime shadows / per-unit cloth sim.

============================================================
MUST NOT
============================================================
- Armor look / greaves / HP invent in this paste
- Root motion that breaks ¼ occupancy
- Pilot / Gasket idle rewrites unless sharing a generic small-unit turn helper (OK to share facing helper if already used by Gasket)
- Solace parts, sprites, PNG blit
- Mixing Aim/laser, floor tiles, New Game

============================================================
QA (one tab)
============================================================
1) Nest Spawn pack: walk a Stripekin — mesh faces the step direction.
2) Attack: spear swings toward target facing, not sideways freeze.
3) Aggro standstill: faces nearest threat (or holds facing into idle).
4) Stand still 3s: idle loop visible (breath/shift); feet stay on ¼.
5) Pack of 3: each can face different dirs; no statue cloud.

Done when: Stripekin faces move/attack/aggro dirs + has a light idle; ¼ grid + chaff role unchanged.
