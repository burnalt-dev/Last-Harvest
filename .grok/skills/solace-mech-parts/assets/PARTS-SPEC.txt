Labor crate — modular part spec (from concept + default south pose)

IDENTITY (lock these)
- Boxy riveted steel crate torso, not Gundam, not Master Chief
- Cyan visor slit, one caution-yellow stripe
- Piston walker legs, dark 1px SNES outline, chunky pixels
- 3/4 top-down, same foot line on every facing

SLOTS
- head: visor / sensor block sitting on the crate lid
- body: crate hull + yellow stripe (back plate lives on the hull, does not animate)
- legs: piston (default) | hover | reverse-joint leap
- armR: weapon hand
- armL: shield on blade kit; empty on two-hand scythe/cannon
- back: backpack / reactor (stat + skills, no pose change)
- aux1/aux2: CPU/programs, no silhouette

STARTER WEAPONS (complete kits, 4 dir)
- blade: one door-slab shield LEFT, one short sword RIGHT. Never two shields.
- scythe: ONE two-hand scythe. South/north = vertical pole in the right hand. East/west = full sweep.
- cannon: ONE two-hand cannon. South = barrel toward camera on the right. Never a mirrored second gun.

SHEET RULES
- Magenta #FF00FF background
- Entire figure including weapons inside the cell with margin
- Overlap onto neighboring tiles at draw time is OK
- A hard cut on the cell edge is a fail
- Same crate scale on down / left / right / up
- Left may be a flip of right if west is not authored
